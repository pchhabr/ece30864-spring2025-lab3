Q1: 

Pros of using Agile Development :

Flexibility & Adaptability – Agile allows for iterative development, making it easier to adapt to changing requirements.
Faster Delivery & Feedback – Frequent releases ensure quicker feedback loops, reducing the risk of building something misaligned with user needs.

Cons of using Agile development :

Less Predictability – Because requirements evolve, it’s harder to predict timelines and budgets compared to a structured approach like Waterfall.
Higher Dependency on Team Collaboration – Agile requires constant communication; without it, progress can slow down or lead to misalignment.

Q2: 

Product Owner:
Sets direction for the team, handles release management and stakeholder management.
Balances Business & Technical Needs – Works closely with the development team to ensure feasibility and efficiency.
Accepts or Rejects Work – Reviews completed work to ensure it meets requirements and adds value.

Scrum Master:
All scrum related tasks – JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings, standups all done by the Scrum Master
Promotes Continuous Improvement – Encourages feedback loops and retrospectives to enhance efficiency.
Coaches & Supports the Team – Helps the team adopt Agile best practices and improve collaboration.
